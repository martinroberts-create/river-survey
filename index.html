Does the river follow Bradshaw?

When several groups measured the same site:

Each variable is tested against distance from source with Spearman's rank correlation (rs). The critical value is at the 95% level, one-tailed, because the model predicts the direction of change. You need at least 5 sites.

Circles are natural sites. Squares are sites with a bridge, weir or reinforced banks, which can explain results that don't fit the pattern.

Spread of the bed load at each site

Box plots show the whole sample, not just the average. The box covers the middle half of the pieces (the interquartile range), the line inside is the median, the dot is the mean, and the whiskers reach the smallest and largest pieces. A narrowing box downstream means the load is becoming more sorted.

Bed load roundness at each site

Each bar shows what share of the pieces collected at a site fell into each Powers category. Sites run from source (top) to mouth (bottom). If Bradshaw is right, the rounder colours should take up more of each bar downstream.
